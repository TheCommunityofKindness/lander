## 💛 What Is the Community of Kindness?

Community of Kindness is a **local-first, dignity-first** model for community support.  
It focuses on:

- Reducing social isolation  
- Providing practical help (food, transport, connection, care)  
- Empowering volunteers and neighbours  
- Building resilient, self-sustaining community networks  
- Supporting vulnerable people without bureaucracy or stigma  

The first pilot program — **Lunchies** — demonstrates how small, consistent acts of care can transform lives.
